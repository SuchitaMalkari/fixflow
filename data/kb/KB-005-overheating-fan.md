# Overheating, loud fan or laptop is hot

## Symptoms
The fan is loud or always running, the laptop is hot on the bottom or keyboard, or it slows down and shuts off when busy.

## Quick fix
1. Use the laptop on a hard, flat surface. Beds, sofas and laps block the air vents.
2. Check Task Manager for processes using high CPU, such as browser tabs or stuck updates.
3. In the Device Settings app, set Thermal Mode to Quiet or Balanced instead of Performance.
4. Update the BIOS, which often includes fan-curve fixes.
5. Clean the vents with short bursts of compressed air while the laptop is off.

## When to open a ticket
If the laptop shuts down from heat, the fan makes grinding or clicking noises, or the fan never spins, Hardware Repair will inspect the cooling system. A burning smell is a safety issue: follow KB-003.
