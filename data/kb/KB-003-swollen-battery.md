# Swollen battery, burning smell, smoke or sparks

## Symptoms
The case is bulging or the touchpad is pushed up, the laptop no longer sits flat, there is a burning or chemical smell, smoke, hissing, sparks, or the device is too hot to touch.

## What to do immediately
1. Stop using the device. Unplug the charger if it is safe to do so.
2. Do not charge it, press on it, or try to remove the battery yourself.
3. Move it away from anything flammable to a hard, non-flammable surface.
4. Do not ship it with regular mail. Lithium batteries that are damaged need special handling.

## Escalation
This is a safety issue. A support specialist will contact you directly to arrange a safe pickup or replacement. These cases are always handled by a person, never closed automatically.
