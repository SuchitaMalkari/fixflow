# Laptop won't turn on, won't boot or shows a blue screen

## Symptoms
The laptop does not power on, shows the logo then restarts, is stuck in an update loop or automatic repair, or shows a blue screen error (BSOD).

## Quick fix
1. Connect the original charger and leave it for 30 minutes, then hold the power button for 15 seconds and try again.
2. If the power light comes on but the screen stays black, connect an external monitor.
3. For update loops or automatic repair, choose Advanced options > Startup Repair, or Uninstall latest quality update.
4. For blue screens, write down the stop code (for example IRQL_NOT_LESS_OR_EQUAL) and boot into Safe Mode to remove recently installed drivers or software.
5. If BitLocker asks for a recovery key, get it from your work account portal before trying anything else.

## When to open a ticket
If the laptop shows no lights at all or blue screens keep coming back, open a ticket. Mention the stop code. If you may lose important data, say so: a specialist will help before any reset.
