# Battery not charging or stuck at a percentage

## Symptoms
The laptop is plugged in but the battery percentage does not go up, shows "plugged in, not charging", or always stops at 60% or 80%.

## Quick fix
1. Check whether Battery Conservation Mode (also called Charge Threshold or Battery Care) is on in the Device Settings app. When it is on, charging stops at 60% or 80% on purpose to extend battery life. Turn it off if you need a full charge.
2. Use the original USB-C or barrel charger. Low-wattage phone chargers (under 45W) may not charge the laptop at all.
3. Unplug the charger, shut down, hold the power button for 15 seconds, then plug back in and power on.
4. Update the BIOS and the power-management driver from the Updates page.

## When to open a ticket
If the battery never charges with the original adapter after these steps, the adapter, port or battery may be faulty. Hardware Repair will run a battery health test.
