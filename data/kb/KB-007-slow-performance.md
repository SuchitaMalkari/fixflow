# Laptop is slow, freezing or apps not responding

## Symptoms
The laptop takes a long time to start, apps freeze, the system feels slow, or disk usage stays at 100%.

## Quick fix
1. Restart the laptop. Fast Startup can keep the system running for weeks without a real restart.
2. Open Task Manager > Startup apps and disable apps you do not need at startup.
3. Check free disk space. Keep at least 15% of the drive free; use Storage Sense to clear temporary files.
4. Install pending Windows and driver updates, then restart.
5. Run a full antivirus scan.

## When to open a ticket
If the laptop is still slow after these steps or the drive reports errors, Software Support can check for a corrupted system image or a failing drive.
