# Wi-Fi keeps disconnecting, slow Wi-Fi or Bluetooth problems

## Symptoms
Wi-Fi drops every few minutes, the network is not listed, the internet is very slow on one laptop only, VPN will not connect, or Bluetooth headphones and mice disconnect.

## Quick fix
1. Turn Airplane mode on and off. Forget the network and join it again.
2. Run Settings > Network > Network troubleshooter.
3. Update the wireless (WLAN) and Bluetooth drivers from the Updates page.
4. In Device Manager > Network adapters > Wireless adapter > Power Management, untick "Allow the computer to turn off this device to save power".
5. For the office VPN, check the date and time are correct and that you are signed in to the VPN client with your work account.

## When to open a ticket
If several people in the same office lose Wi-Fi or VPN at the same time, it is a network outage: Network Operations will investigate.
