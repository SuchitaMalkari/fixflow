# Liquid spill, drop damage, cracked screen or broken hinge

## Symptoms
Liquid was spilled on the laptop, it was dropped, the screen is cracked, a hinge is loose or broken, or a port is physically damaged.

## Quick fix
1. For a liquid spill, shut down immediately, unplug the charger, and turn the laptop upside down in a tent shape to drain. Do not turn it on to test it.
2. Do not use a hair dryer or rice. Let a technician check it.
3. For cracked screens or broken hinges, stop opening and closing the lid to avoid more damage.
4. Back up your files from cloud sync if the laptop still works.

## When to open a ticket
All physical damage goes to Hardware Repair. Accidental damage is covered only on Premium Care plans; on Base plans the repair is quoted before any work is done.
