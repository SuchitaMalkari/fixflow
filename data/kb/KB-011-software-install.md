# Installing software, licences and app errors

## Symptoms
You need a new application installed, an app asks for admin rights, a licence has expired, or Office or another app shows an activation or crash error.

## Quick fix
1. Open the Company Portal (Software Center) app and search for the application. Approved apps install without admin rights.
2. For Office activation errors, sign out of all Office apps, restart, and sign in again with your work account.
3. Repair the app from Settings > Apps > Installed apps > the app > Advanced options > Repair.
4. Clear the app's cache or reinstall it from the Company Portal.

## When to open a ticket
Apps that are not in the Company Portal need a software request reviewed by Software Support. Include the business reason and the licence type you need.
