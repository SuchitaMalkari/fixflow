# Battery drains quickly

## Symptoms
Battery life is much shorter than usual, the laptop loses charge while asleep, or the battery percentage drops suddenly.

## Quick fix
1. Open Settings > System > Power and switch the power mode to Balanced or Best power efficiency.
2. Lower screen brightness and turn off keyboard backlight when not needed.
3. Check Task Manager > Processes for apps with high CPU or "Very high" power usage and close them.
4. Turn off "Wake on LAN" and "USB charging when off" in the BIOS if the battery drains while the lid is closed.
5. Run the Battery Health report in the Device Settings app. A battery under 70% health is worn out.

## When to open a ticket
A battery below 70% health that is still under warranty can be replaced by Hardware Repair.
