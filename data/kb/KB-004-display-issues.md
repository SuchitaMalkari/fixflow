# Screen flickering, black screen or no external display

## Symptoms
The screen flickers, shows lines or wrong colours, stays black while the laptop is on, or an external monitor is not detected.

## Quick fix
1. Press Windows + Ctrl + Shift + B to restart the graphics driver.
2. Connect an external monitor. If the external monitor works, the internal panel or its cable may be faulty.
3. For external monitors, press Windows + P and choose Extend or Duplicate. Try a different cable and port, and confirm the USB-C port supports DisplayPort (look for the DP or lightning symbol).
4. Update the graphics driver and BIOS from the Updates page.
5. Turn off adaptive brightness and variable refresh rate in display settings if the flicker changes with content.

## When to open a ticket
Lines on the screen, a cracked panel, or a black internal screen that works on an external monitor need Hardware Repair.
