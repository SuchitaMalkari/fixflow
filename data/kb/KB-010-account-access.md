# Password reset, account locked or MFA not working

## Symptoms
You forgot your password, your work account is locked, you cannot sign in to Windows or email, or the multi-factor authentication (MFA) app is on a lost or new phone.

## Quick fix
1. Use the self-service password reset portal from another device. You will need your registered phone number or backup email.
2. Accounts lock for 15 minutes after 5 failed attempts. Wait, then sign in with the correct password.
3. Check Caps Lock and the keyboard language on the sign-in screen.
4. If you changed your password recently, connect to the office network or VPN so the laptop learns the new password.

## When to open a ticket
A lost MFA device, an account that stays locked, or a suspected compromised account (sign-ins you do not recognise) must go to the IT Helpdesk. Report a suspected compromise immediately.
